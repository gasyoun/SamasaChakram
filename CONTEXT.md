# Samāsa diagram language

These terms describe how a Sanskrit compound's reading is represented in a diagram.

## Language

**Ladder**:
An ordered reading of a directed compound. The reading starts at the head and follows the attested attachments toward the left.
_Avoid_: Pyramid

**Attachment rung**:
A step of the ladder that records a single directed grammatical attachment between two members or groups. An attested inner attachment stays distinct from the attachment that joins its group to the rest of the compound.
_Avoid_: Side branch, parallel tier

**Dvandva**:
A coordinate compound whose members are equal. Its coordinate relation has no succession of higher and lower members and is the exception to the ladder form.
_Avoid_: Ranked chain of conjuncts

**Syntactic head**:
The member that anchors a directed reading. It is the first item read on the ladder.
_Avoid_: Referent

**External referent**:
The person or thing denoted by a bahuvrīhi, such as the possessor in “one whose wealth is five cows.” It lies outside the written compound.
_Avoid_: An additional written member

**Semantic step**:
A visibly distinct step after the grammatical attachments of a bahuvrīhi. It asks “whose?” and identifies the external referent without adding a written member or a grammatical attachment.
_Avoid_: Extra attachment rung
