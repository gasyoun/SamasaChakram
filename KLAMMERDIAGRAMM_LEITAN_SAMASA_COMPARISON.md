## 1 · Where the two systems agree — the load-bearing principles

| Principle | Leitan (conspectus) | /klammerdiagramm |
|---|---|---|
| **Binary constituency** | «элементов в композите может быть от двух до нескольких десятков, а **частей (не считая двандву и отдельные исключения) — всегда две**»; pūrvapada : uttarapada; a reviewer note [ak] even asks to spell out the recursion of parts | every node binds exactly two constituents; recursion via composite children — the same statement, drawn |
| **Right-to-left resolution** | «начинать переводить татпурушу следует **обязательно справа налево**»; his stanza ladder indents right-to-left, deepest last | the MG law: every samāsa binds right-to-left — tatpuruṣa, karmadhāraya, bahuvrīhi, avyayībhāva alike |
| **Dvandva апарт** | «не считая двандву» — the sole named exception to two-parts; ubhayapadārtha-pradhāna | the sole exception to the law: flat band, no internal head |
| **Prominence ≠ structure** | Patañjali's pradhāna scheme (avyayībhāva pūrva-, tatpuruṣa uttara-, bahuvrīhi anya-, dvandva ubhaya-) presented as the *semantic* classification | our rulings: exocentricity (bahuvrīhi) and pūrvapada-prominence (avyayībhāva) are semantics; the bracket direction never changes |
| **Same word, different samāsa** | the lokanātha joke (bahuvrīhi для нищего, ṣaṣṭhī-tatpuruṣa для царя) and the hatadvipa four-way polysemy | identical drawing, different gloss — structure and semantics deliberately separated, exactly what the joke exploits |
| **Karmadhāraya ⊂ tatpuruṣa** | karmadhāraya = samānādhikaraṇa-tatpuruṣa | drawn as the same directed pair as any tatpuruṣa |

## 5 · Side-findings worth keeping

- The conspectus contains **the uddāma stanza itself** with full translation — the plate
  compound's source is Jalhaṇa's *Sūktimuktāvalī* (13 c.), via Leitan's webinar handout.
  The gallery can now cite the source text.
- His indentation ladders are typographic Klammerdiagramme without the brackets — the two
  systems are the same method at different levels of formalization, which is why the
  crosswalk closed without grammar changes.
- The kevala-samāsa category (ājanmaśuddha, vāgarthāviva) and aluk compounds (yudhiṣṭhira)
  confirm the value of our tokens-as-surface-members design: retained case endings and
  frozen particles draw without special handling.

## 6 · "Same method, different formalization" — how different, precisely

The two systems are **duals**: a compound has two axes — its *constituency* (the binary
nesting) and its *classification* (the Pāṇinian/semantic type) — and each system fully
formalizes exactly ONE axis and leaves the other implicit.

| Axis | Leitan's conspectus | /klammerdiagramm |
|---|---|---|
| **Constituency** (binary nesting) | *implicit* — carried by right-to-left **indentation ladders** and prose vigraha; no bars, brackets, node labels, or part-spans; ambiguity is resolvable only by reading the prose | *fully formal* — an explicit `nodes:[label,left,right]` binary structure, machine-rendered as the SVG plate; right-to-left is a geometric **invariant**, not a reader instruction; machine-checkable and machine-drawable |
| **Classification** (Pāṇinian type) | *fully formal* — names every subtype (vibhakti classes, dvigu's three conditions, the sixteen avyayībhāva senses, both bahuvrīhi case-classes); a *classificatory* grammar telling you WHAT KIND | *deliberately absent* — the drawing never encodes bahuvrīhi-vs-tatpuruṣa; class is pushed to annotation/semantics (the lokanātha joke exploits exactly this separation) |

So "how different" has an exact answer: **they formalize opposite axes.** Leitan's ladder is a
Klammerdiagramm with the brackets replaced by whitespace and the *type-label* promoted to
first-class; the plate is Leitan's ladder with the type-label demoted to a footnote and the
*bracket geometry* promoted to first-class. Direction is the tell — for Leitan it is a
*procedure* the reader performs («переводить справа налево»); for the plate it is a
*structural law* baked into the geometry (every node binds right-to-left; dvandva flat).

**Why this matters for the crosswalk.** Because the two formalized axes are orthogonal,
Leitan's taxonomy *cannot* contradict the constituency — it lives on the axis the drawing
omits. That orthogonality is the reason the crosswalk closed with **zero grammar changes**,
and it locates every friction precisely: D1–D6 all sit on the *join* between the axes —
places where his taxonomy *implies* a constituency detail the plate must decide how to render
(dvigu placement, samāhāra collectivity, first-part dominance, an elided member, the uddāma
bracketing, the śītoṣṇa class). Resolving them (§4) is exactly the act of stitching the two
formalizations along their shared seam.

**Can we draw all his examples?** Yes, structurally — §2 shows every category maps onto the
seven forms, and three of his own (including his hardest, the seven-lemma Jalhaṇa stanza
ladder) were drawn zero-code. The one caveat is the same duality: the plate draws all his
*compounds* but does not render his *type-labels* (those ride as annotation, by design). Which
model tier suffices to reuse the drawing skill is answered in the skill doc — Sonnet to draw
an obvious nesting, Opus to decide a contested one.
