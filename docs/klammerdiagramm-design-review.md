## 1. pañca-gava-dhanaḥ

- **Attestation:** the [Kāśikā on Pāṇini 2.1.51](https://textgridrep.de/browse/49kdq.0) gives *pañcagavadhanaḥ* as an uttarapada example. Tubb and Boose, *Scholastic Sanskrit*, §1.56.2, p. 110, and the [Leitan comparison §3.2](https://github.com/gasyoun/SamasaChakram/blob/main/KLAMMERDIAGRAMM_LEITAN_SAMASA_COMPARISON.md) give the possessive analysis, “one whose wealth is five cows.”
- **Confirmed in Q1:** `pañca → gava` and `[pañca-gava] → dhana` are two successive attachment rungs **on one ladder**. The first bond forms the numeral–noun group; the second attaches that intact group to `dhana`. This order describes how the groups are formed. The head-first reading starts at `dhana`, the outer anchor.
- **Never:** do not replace the attested grouping with `pañca → [gava-dhana]`, show the two attachments as parallel branches, or turn the outside possessor into a written member.
- **Confirmed in Q2:** add a visibly distinct semantic step after the two attachment rungs: **Whose?** → “one whose wealth is five cows.” This follows the [samāsa ladder trainer](https://gasyoun.github.io/SanskritGrammar/grammars/sangram/articles/samasa-ladder/). The possessor is outside the written compound.

### First drawing: confirmed acceptance sketch (Q3)

Keep the written word on one line, with the head `dhana` on the right. Use a single staircase for the two attachments. When checking the reading, start from `dhana` and keep the dependent `[pañca-gava]` intact; the inner rung explains its composition.

```text
Written members      pañca — gava — dhanaḥ
Head to read first                    dhana
Attachment rung      pañca → gava
Attachment rung      [pañca-gava] → dhana
Semantic step        Whose? → one whose wealth is five cows
```

The sketch lists attachment order independently of head-first reading order. Do not turn the lines into a branching tree, split off `gava-dhana`, or put the possessor on the written word line. Keep the existing I/II division at `dhanaḥ`. Q3 confirms drawing this one example for visual review. Replacing the gallery asset or redrawing other examples is not part of this check.
